# Spectra viewer

Spectra's viewer: a React app built with Vite. The `a2ui-spectra` tool in [`../spectra/`](../spectra) copies its build and serves it, with the API and the live stream its README describes.

## The page

The page follows the design canvas "Spectra viewer UI": https://claude.ai/artifact/EiYG8HSHhGhPfDkMaRexQ2. A left rail holds Spectra's mark and name, the Turns section and, at its foot, the Keys button and the theme: System, Light or Dark, remembered in the browser. A context bar shows the agent's name, the mode, whether the page hears Spectra's live stream, and the wire proxy's address with a copy button. The agent's name opens a popover with the agent's URL, every A2A version and A2UI extension its card states, each extension with its catalogs, a basic catalog by its short name, and the mode. Under it, the turn list and the turn view sit side by side as panels, with a divider between them. Every divider can be dragged, and the browser remembers where: the turn list's width, the timeline's width, the step's detail's height when open, and the Preview pane's width beside Structure at each preview width, the same for every turn. The detail opens at its remembered height, at most half the height it shares with the result; as the window is resized, the result takes the change.

- **The selection** is in the page address, as `#/turns/<trace id>`, followed by `/steps/<step id>` while a step is selected, picked or landed on, and by `/nodes/<surface id>/<component id>`, each encoded, while a tree node is selected. Changing the turn adds an entry to the browser's history; changing the step or the node, or the viewer's own move, replaces it, so Back walks through the turns viewed, each on the step it was left on. A reload brings back the node, its parse and its tinted range; a node no longer in the tree shown is dropped. An address whose step is not in its closed turn opens the turn as one with no step does, and drops the step. While the newest turn is selected, a new turn from any host is selected as it opens; selecting an older turn stops this, and selecting the newest again resumes it. A turn sent from the composer is selected as it opens. A page opened with no turn in its address selects the newest.
- **A small window**, below 1,280px wide, has a strip in place of the turn list: one status icon per turn, in the list's order, each selecting its turn, with a red dot on a turn with problems and its input and problem count as its tooltip. The strip's button opens the full list over the turn view; the arrows select as they move through it, and a turn pressed, Enter on the turn reached, Esc, a press outside it or the button again closes it. The page is at least 740px wide, and scrolls sideways below that.
- **The turn list collapses** to the same strip at any width, with the button at the right of its header, remembered in the browser. On a wide window the strip's button expands it back in place, and so does `/`. It slides between its width and the strip's in 200ms, easing out: the list, held at its width so its text never reflows, is clipped to the card's shape as the panel narrows and revealed as it widens, while the strip fades in over it where it lands, or away as it opens; the turn view's content is held at the width it lands at, so it lays out once, its card revealing or covering it. The list stays mounted while collapsed, hidden under the strip, so opening it replays nothing and keeps its search and open groups; it and the strip scroll the selected turn into view within their own column, never sideways. It switches at once when the system asks for reduced motion; dragging the divider never collapses it.
- **The keys.** The turn list, the strip and the timeline are each one Tab stop, and the up and down arrows select the turn or the step they land on. In the turn list they land on the group headers too, the right arrow opens a group and the left closes it, or moves from a turn to its header. In the timeline, Home selects the first row and End the last, following a live turn again. `n` and Shift+`n` go to the next and previous problem of the turn, wrapping around; `l` jumps to the latest step; `m` maximizes the step's detail or restores the split; `/` focuses the search, opening the full list on a small window; `?` and the Keys button list the keys. A key of one character does nothing while typing in a field, or with Ctrl, Cmd or Alt held.
- **The first screen**, with no turns, shows the agent's name and the live status, the first example of each of its card's skills as prompts that fill the composer, unless the message box cannot send to the agent, and the wire proxy's address to point a host app at. Under `attach` it adds the variables to set in the agent's shell, as `NAME=value` lines, with one Copy button that copies them as `export` lines. The composer is focused.
- **The turn list** shows the connected agent's turns, by the name on its card; when the store holds turns of other agents, from earlier runs in the same folder, the agent picker in its header names the agent shown and offers each agent with turns, the connected one first and marked "connected", each with its number of turns, then every agent together. Picking an agent selects its newest turn; a turn selected by a link, the browser's history or the composer that the list does not show brings its agent's turns, or every agent's for a turn that names none. The pick holds until the page is reloaded. The list groups the turns by conversation, newest first. Each group is titled by its conversation's first message and its agent, with its number of turns and problems, and opens and closes; a turn with no conversation is a group of its own. Each row shows the turn's status, its time, its input in brief, its duration and its problem count. The search, which `/` focuses, matches a turn's input, its conversation's title and agent, and a trace or conversation id, whole or by its start. A group that gains the selection opens, even one closed by hand.
- **The turn's header** leads with the turn's input. Beside it are the outcome and the check state: the number of problems, "No problems", or "Not checked" and why, with each check that ran, its protocol version and its result in a tooltip. One line below holds the start, the duration, the A2UI version, then, on a turn with A2UI step events, the inference formats its prompt generations and parses used and the catalogs its prompt generations were made on, each once in order of first use, the basic catalog named as the agent's popover names it, then the agent, and the trace and conversation ids with copy buttons. A turn with a model call or a tool call has a second quiet line, growing on a live turn as its calls end: the time from the turn's start to the first model call's first chunk, or its end when it did not stream or is read from spans only; to the first A2UI message; the number of model calls, raw ones and those read from spans only included; the number of retries among them, when there are any; the number of tool calls, counting the full tool rows and leaving housekeeping out; the input and output tokens summed over the calls with usage, those read from spans only included, as `9.1k` above a thousand; the share read from the cache, when the turn read any; the estimated cost of the priced calls, to two significant figures and at most four decimals, with the price table's date, or "Prices from the config" when every priced call used the config's price; how many calls have no price; and how many were recorded raw.
- **The timeline** lists the turn's steps and calls by start time: the input, each status update, text part, data part, file part and A2UI message, each model call and tool call, and the outcome last, each with its own icon; an offset or a duration of a minute or more reads `1m 27s` in the timeline's columns and in the spans section's events. A call a proxy recorded and the received spans linked to it are one row; nested received spans of one kind with no exchange, such as ADK's `call_llm` around its `generate_content`, are one row too, read from spans only; framework spans have no rows. A model call's icon takes its provider's colour, on its row and its detail's title line: Gemini's for Gemini's API, and for Google's on a Gemini model, in a gradient of red `#F94543`, yellow `#FABC12`, green `#08B962` and blue `#3186FF`, the colours Lobe Icons' Gemini mark blends; Claude's for Anthropic's, in `#D97757`, the colour Simple Icons records for Claude; OpenAI's in the text colour, since OpenAI's brand guidelines add no colour to its mark, and only for a call to OpenAI's own host, as other servers speak OpenAI's APIs too; any other provider's, or a call recorded raw, stays grey. A model call's row is named by the model id as requested, with the provider as its target, or by the host, with the method and path, for a call recorded raw; one read from spans only adds its source, `Gemini · ADK spans`, or its source alone when its spans name no provider. A tool call's row is named by the tool for `tools/call` and by the method otherwise, with "MCP" and the server's host; by the method and the path, with "HTTP" and the host, for an HTTP tool; and by the tool, with "in the agent", when read from spans only. An MCP request other than `tools/call`, `resources/read` and `prompts/get`, a `DELETE`, a standalone `response`, and a server's notifications and requests are housekeeping rows, as small and quiet as a delivery's. A parse of the A2UI step is a row, "Parse" with its inference format and "streamed" for a stream, with a bar from its start to its end; the toolset's `send_a2ui_json_to_client` tool call joins its parse's row, "Parse · send_a2ui_json_to_client · Direct JSON", when the parse sits under the tool's span or shares its call id, the row addressed by the proxy's span id, else the parse's; a prompt generation has a row only when it failed, "Prompt generation" with its format. A model call that is a retry carries a grey "Retry 1" tag before the problem mark's column: in a turn, the first model call that starts after a failed parse ends is that parse's retry, counted along the chain, a failure, retry 1, a failure, retry 2; a retry the producer states with `a2ui.retry.attempt` wins, with the parse its link names. Selecting a parse shows the surfaces as they stood after the last wire message traced to it, or right after the parse when none is. A call's step in the page address is its proxy's span id, else its innermost span's; when a row's id changes, as a span-only row merges into its proxy's row once their link arrives, the selection and the page address follow it. Selecting a call shows the surfaces as they stood at its end, or the latest state while it is in flight. Every row ends in a narrow time lane, drawn against the length of the whole turn, from its start to the latest of its end and every row's end, the present on a live turn: a call's bar, lighter until a streamed model call's first token and darker after, a tool call's and a call's read from spans only in the darker tone throughout, with its duration beside the lane; a step's or a delivery's dot at its moment. The lane is grey, teal on the selected row and red on a row with a problem; an in-flight call's duration counts up and its bar grows. As the timeline narrows, the target gives way first, then the name; the problem mark, the duration and the lane keep their width. A delivery that carries several steps has a row of its own with the steps under it. Each step with a problem carries a red mark and its count. While the turn is open, the timeline is marked Live and follows its newest step, and the result shows the latest state; picking a step stops following, and Jump to latest resumes it. When a turn the view followed closes, the view lands on its first problem, or on its final state when it has none.
- **The problems** are the findings of the four checks, each on the message its path names; the messages web core's message processor rejects; a failed call, one problem on its row whichever of its sources failed: a model call's first line saying the status the model API answered, the error the stream carried, that the provider could not be reached, or that the connection closed before the response ended, its second line the code, the provider's own status and the API; a tool call's saying the tool server answered a JSON-RPC error or an HTTP status, could not be reached, that the agent cancelled the request or that the connection closed before it was answered, its second line the code and "MCP" or "HTTP"; a call read from spans only saying the agent's span ended in error, with its source; and the third line the status message recorded, a failed span's joining the proxy's; each error a parse raised, one problem on the parse's row, "The parse stopped on this block: …" for the error that ended the parse, "The stream rejected this block and read on: …" for a block a stream read past, "The parse stopped: …" for an error placed in no block, and "The parse failed: …" for a failed parse that states no exception, the second line the error's class and the SDK, "A2uiIntegrityError · A2UI SDK, Python", and "Show text" bringing the rejected block's red-soft mark into view; a failed prompt generation, "The SDK could not write the prompt's UI section: …", on its row; a failed turn's reason, on the outcome; and, while the turn is in view, what its preview page reports. The turn list counts the failed calls and the A2UI step's errors the listing gives. A retry is marked with the error that caused it and is not counted; the tool span under a toolset parse is not counted beside the parse's errors. A tool's own `isError` shows in its Result and is not a problem; a failed framework span is not either. A finding that names no message is on the outcome too. A render error counts in the header, on the turn's row and in the surface switcher, marks its step and is reached with `n`; "Problems only" keeps every step a render error has been on while the turn is in view. It is not part of the record, nor of where a turn lands.
- **The result** shows the agent's reply text in a strip, then the Preview and Structure panes side by side, each shown or hidden by its toggle in the pane's header, both on by default, with a divider between them; the toggles are remembered in the browser. Both show the selected surface as it stood right after the step selected. Structure is the surface's tree above its data model. The agent's A2UI messages of the conversation's earlier turns, then the turn's own up to the step, go one by one into upstream web core's message processor in its relaxed validation mode, with the v0.9 and v1.0 basic catalogs registered and a stand-in catalog with no component schemas under any other catalog id the messages name. Each tree node opens to its properties with its chevron; clicking its row selects it, which selects the parse it came from, opening its detail with the component's range tinted and scrolled into view, the node staying selected and the surface in view following it: a wire message is traced to the latest parse of the turn that started before it was sent and holds an equal message, and failing that a component to the latest such parse holding a component of the same surface and id; a node traced to no parse is selected and nothing else happens. A child id no component has shows as missing; the components no parent reaches are listed under "Not under the root". A surface with a component of a stand-in catalog lists its components in the order they came instead, since its catalog's schemas, which say which properties hold child ids, are not known. The surface switcher lists the surfaces the turn touched, then those it did not change, each with its catalog id and its problems, and the surfaces the turn deleted.
- **The preview** draws the surface with its catalog's own renderer, in a page loaded in an iframe: the config's preview pages in config order, then Spectra's built-in page for the basic catalogs. Every page is loaded at connect and stays loaded, laid over the pane's frame when a surface is routed to it. A page says when it is ready which catalogs it renders; the surface goes to the first page whose claims cover the catalogs its messages name at its protocol version, matched with web core's compatibility rule, a page still loading ahead of it holding the surface until it answers. The page gets every message that names the surface, the conversation's earlier turns' and the turn's own up to the step, and keeps up with a live turn. The frame is Narrow, 384px, or Wide, the pane's width, switched in the header and remembered in the browser, and keeps the page's own background in both themes; the header names the page drawing it. A strip above the frame lists what the page reported. A surface no page claims shows "No preview for catalog" and its id, with a line for each page that has not answered in time, or speaks another version of the protocol; such a page is reloaded every few seconds until it answers. A component that failed to draw is a problem on the step whose message carried it, marking the line of the component's id; a message the page refused, on that message's step; an error web core reported, on the component's step or the outcome. A refused message or a web core error on a step that already has a problem of the viewer's own folds under it as a quiet line, as web core's rejections fold under findings, and a refused message of an earlier turn, which that turn counts, folds on the outcome. The page's controls take the pane's place in the Tab order: Tab from the pane's header enters the page, and Tab past its last control, or Shift+Tab past its first, comes back beside the pane.
- **The step's detail**, in a split under the result that can be resized and collapsed, and collapsed to its title line until a step is selected or the view lands on a problem, shows the step's problems, then what it holds: an A2UI message or a data part as JSON, a text part as text, a file part's name, MIME type and size or URI, each with its part's metadata. It ends with the delivery the step arrived in, folded. A delivery's row shows the delivery as the turn record holds it: its parts in order and its metadata. The Input step shows the host's message, its capabilities and its data model. Every JSON block has a copy button and folds its objects and arrays. Every labelled block of a detail, a text, a JSON block, keys and values, a list, folds on its label line, and a block folded stays folded by its label on every step for the session, so a long system prompt folded once stays out of the way on every call. Maximize, on every step's detail and with `m`, gives the detail the result pane's height, the pane folded to its header line; selecting another step keeps it, and dragging the divider, collapsing the detail or a reload brings the split back.
- **A model call's detail** has a title line with the model id, the API and where the call was seen, the model proxy and the agent's spans linked to it, then its start, duration and time to the first token. Under it: the usage, Input, Output, Reasoning and Cache read on every API and Cache write on Anthropic's, with the hit ratio, the share of the input read from the cache, and the estimated cost, noted "LiteLLM prices · <date>", "Price from the config" or "No price for <model>"; the prefix break's sentence, against the earlier call of the same model in the conversation that shares the longest prefix, this turn's or an earlier turn's, with the earlier call's time a link that selects it; on a retry, before the prefix break's sentence, a grey sentence saying why, "Retry 1 · the reply of the call at +2.0 s failed to parse · Surface default already exists", "failed to parse" a link that selects the failed parse; the request, its settings, system prompt, tool definitions and messages, with a grey marker where the shared prefix ends and the earlier and this call's differing lines under it, and the UI section the A2UI SDK generated kept in its place in the system prompt or a message as a bounded region, folded by default behind "Generated by the A2UI SDK · Express · basic, v1.0", unfolded on a faint neutral tint ending in a thin line, every occurrence marked, the prefix break's marker falling inside a section opening it, found as a string of the request that holds the whole section text; the response, filling as chunks arrive while the call is in flight, under it one link per parse that read the call, "Read by the parse at +4.8 s · Express", or, on a turn with A2UI messages but no A2UI step events, the grey sentence "No A2UI step recorded · switch the wrapper on to see which text became which component", under `attach` adding the variables on the first screen, with a folded "How to switch it on" of the two packages' install and launch lines with Copy; and the exchange, folded, with its headers, a removed credential as "Removed by Spectra", its bodies and its chunks. In the messages and the response, a part with nothing in it, text or reasoning that is empty, has no block, and a message of such parts alone is its label line with "empty text" or "empty reasoning". A call recorded raw says so in one sentence and opens on its exchange. A call read from spans only, or one whose proxy side was recorded raw, fills its usage, request and response from its chain's spans, each name from the innermost span that carries it and the token counts together from one span, each section's label adding "· from ADK spans"; with no content in its spans, one grey sentence takes the request's place. Its provider and price come from the chain too: `gcp.gemini` shows as Gemini, `gcp.gen_ai` as Google, both priced as `gemini`, a LiteLLM span's `gemini/` model id meeting its own key; the title line names the API the provider implies. It has no prefix break. The estimated cost and the prefix break are computed in the viewer, with the price table read once from `GET /api/prices`.
- **A tool call's detail** has a title line with the tool's name or the method, "MCP" or "HTTP" and the host, or "in the agent", where the call was seen, and its start and duration. Under it: Arguments as JSON, a housekeeping request's params in their place; Result, a text block as text, an empty one with no block, and every other block as JSON, a resource's contents with its media type and URI, labelled "Error" for the tool's own `isError` or a JSON-RPC error; the exchange, folded on MCP, its line naming the server's `GET` stream by its path, `reply on GET /sse`, when the reply came on it, and open on an HTTP tool; and MCP's names, the method, the protocol version, the session and the request id. A call read from spans only takes its arguments and result from its span. In flight, it shows the arguments from the call's start, the result at its end and the exchange's chunks as they arrive.
- **A parse's detail** has a title line with "Parse", its format, "streamed" and its scope, "a2ui-spectra-instrumentation 0.1.0", or "A2UI step spans" without one, then its start and duration. Under its problems: "Read from", the model call the parse read as a link, "The reply of gemini-3.6-flash at +2.0 s", or "Tied to no model call", and, on a failed parse that was retried, "Retried by the call at +5.0 s", a link that selects the retry; the raw text, open, mono, plain at rest, each rejected block in red-soft, with each component's characters worked out by a reader of the parse's inference format, Direct JSON, Express, Elemental or Atom, all four hand-written scanners that find blocks as the pinned agent SDK's lexer does and name components by its compiler's id rule, a range the producer states in `a2ui.parse.components` winning whole: hovering outlines the innermost component under the pointer, clicking selects it, its whole range taking the teal-soft tint and its tree node selected, text outside any component staying plain; a grey sentence above says when the ranges are not all worked out, "No component ranges · the parse names no inference format", "No component ranges · the Express reader could not read this text", or "Component ranges up to character 1,204 · the Express reader stopped there"; the messages the parse produced, folded, those a failed parse produced before its error included; "Returned to the model", what the toolset's tool returned, on its call; and the spans, "From the agent's spans" with the step span's scope as its source line, ADK's span above a toolset parse's. The model call a parse read is the one of its turn whose reply text, or one of whose tool-call arguments, holds the parse's input or is held in it; when several, the one accounting for the most of the input, as a reply the input holds over a tool call's argument the input quotes, then the latest started before the parse; failing that, the latest model call started before the parse, unless two started at once. A failed prompt generation's detail is its problem and its spans.
- **From the agent's spans**, last in a call's detail when a span is linked or read, holds one line per source and scope of the call's chain, such as "ADK spans · gcp.vertex.agent 2.3.0"; "Above this call", every span above the chain within the turn, root first, then the chain itself marked "this call", each with its duration, "· status error" for a span that failed, and folding open to its attributes as received; "Translated", each translated name once, the innermost span's value winning; "Events", the chain's span events in time order, a received log record marked "log record"; and "As received", one folded block per span of the chain with its attribute count.
- **The receiver's late spans**: a `turn_amended` on the live stream replaces or adds each event by its span id in every form of the turn the page holds, its open form, the closed turn and every record that embeds it, and the timeline, the header's second line, the problems and the turn's list row follow. Following a live turn stays on its newest row by start time; a late span with an earlier start does not move it. A turn the page holds in no form has its record read once, and its list row from it. Nothing is announced.
- **The composer**, docked under the turn view, is one line until typing and grows with its text up to six lines; Enter sends, and Shift+Enter starts a new line. It continues the conversation of the turn in view, whichever of its turns is selected, when the message box started it: it names the conversation by its first message, says when the agent is waiting for input, and waits while that conversation's turn is open. A conversation the box did not start in this run, and one whose agent named none, is not continued: sending starts a new conversation, and the composer says so. New conversation starts one from the composer alone, until a turn is selected. A text carries the data model of every surface that asked for it with `sendDataModel`, as the surfaces stood after the step in view, the shown one's from its preview page with what was typed in it.
- **A press in the preview** lands in the composer as a pending action, in place of the text, which stays under it: a line naming the action, its component and its surface, with Copy, Discard and Send, and the wire message the agent would receive, in the turn record's form, its data models folded. It is the A2UI action message in the surface's version, the part's content an object in A2A 0.3 and a list in 1.0, and the data models under their version's key: the pressed surface's from the page, the others' at the step of the render it was pressed in, even after a newer render went out. Focus moves to Send, so Enter sends it; Escape, or Discard, takes it out, and focus goes back to the text. A newer press replaces it, and a turn of another conversation or New conversation drops it. It is sent only in a conversation the message box started, and the composer says why otherwise; a send that fails leaves it pending.
- While the live stream is down, or silent past its heartbeat, the context bar says so; when it comes back, the page reads the listing, the box and the selected turn again.

## Stack

React, with Radix primitives and the resizable panels shadcn/ui wraps, on Tailwind in A2UI Composer's configuration (`components.json`), `@a2ui/web_core` from npm for the surfaces, and the preview protocol's messages from `a2ui-spectra-preview` in [`../../typescript/preview/core/`](../../typescript/preview/core). Inter and JetBrains Mono are bundled from Fontsource, so the page fetches nothing from outside Spectra. Every size, space, radius, line height and letter spacing is a step of Tailwind's scale, with no arbitrary size; the turn list's swing clips with an arbitrary `clip-path` and the strip hides its scrollbar with arbitrary properties, which have no utility. A recurring value with no step is a theme token in `src/index.css`, the selected row's mark `shadow-mark-start`, the selected tab's `shadow-mark-bottom`, and the key columns `grid-cols-facts`, `grid-cols-diff`, `grid-cols-pairs`, `grid-cols-pairs-narrow` and `grid-cols-pairs-wide`.

## Layout

- `src/model/`: what the viewer reads from turn records: the timeline of steps and calls, the call rows joining a proxy's event to its linked spans and a chain of spans into one, the tool calls' rows, arguments, results and failures, the time lane, the problems on them, the check state, the turn list's groups and search, the agents it shows and its picker offers, the message parts with nothing in them, the conversation replay into surfaces, bindings and JSON lines as the tree and the JSON blocks show them, the input in brief, the A2UI messages and where findings point, the model calls with their usage and failures, the estimated cost, the prefix break, the header's second line, the preview's feed, routing and the problems its page reports, the host message the box sends with its data models, the turn's summary, following the newest step and turn, the timeline's keys and the next problem, the turn list's keys, the page's keys, the page address, what the agent's popover reads from the card, the A2UI step with its retries, ties and problems, and the component ranges with the four readers under `readers/`. Each test is beside its source as `*.test.ts`; the readers are proved on every parse of the wrapper capture suite.
- `src/state/`: the page's store, fed by the API and the live stream, what each live event changes in it, `turn_amended` included, with its tests, what each pane reads from it, with the composer's view tested beside it, the theme, the dividers' remembered positions, the panes' toggles, the preview's width and the turn list's collapse, the small window, the preview pages in their iframes, the render errors of the turn in view, and the pending action with the data model at the step in view, tested beside it.
- `src/components/`: the rail, the context bar, the turn list, its strip, the turn view's parts, the Preview pane and the list of keys.
- `src/testing/`: reads the A2A capture suite's recordings, the tool, receiver and wrapper capture suites' records and the turn record fixtures from the conformance workspace for the tests, and builds hand-written model calls, tool calls, received spans, parses and prompt generations.

## Commands

From `tools/spectra_viewer/`:

- `yarn build`: builds the preview core, type-checks the app and builds it into `dist/`.
- `yarn test`: builds the preview core, type-checks the tests and runs them under Node's test runner, on the recordings in `../../conformance/test_data/a2a_capture/`, the tool, receiver and wrapper capture suites' records, and the turn record fixtures the A2UI step's tests read.
- `yarn lint` and `yarn lint:fix`: ESLint with the root ESLint preset and React's hooks rules.
- `yarn format` and `yarn format:check`: Prettier, with the root `.prettierignore`.

From the repository root, `yarn build:all`, `yarn test:all`, `yarn lint:all` and `yarn format:check:all` run the same across every workspace.
